Client rebuild · Roofing · Muskoka

From dated to dominant.

A veteran roofing crew was carrying a website from another era: grey panels, broken image icons, a template shrug. The rebuild leads with what they actually have: standing-seam metalwork over the lakes, photographed the way it deserves. It runs the playbook from our roofing website design guide, applied to a real crew's real work.

  • The crew's own jobsite photography as the full-bleed hero
  • Every roof type gets its own page and its own proof
  • One phone number, pinned to every scroll depth

Demo rebuild · Law firm · North York

(02) Full builds

Three niches, three signatures.

None of these businesses had a website, just booking links, map pins and camera rolls. We designed each build from the business's own material: its photography, its menu, its room. No two of them share a layout.

Demo build · live
Demo build · Specialty café · North York · No website before

Measured to the gram.

A steel-and-black coffee bar that weighs every shot had nothing online but a map pin. The demo borrows the room's discipline: menu-board darkness, one warm line of light, and menu numbers set in mono type.

  • A pinned brew-scale moment that counts the grams as you scroll
  • The menu typeset like the board behind the bar
  • Directions and hours placed up front

Client build · Hair salon · Keremeos, BC · No website before

Forty years, finally online.

A two-chair salon with four decades behind it and no web presence at all. The build keeps the room's character, the slate-blue walls, the wood, the station clock, and puts a working phone number a thumb's reach from every screen.

  • A palette pulled from the salon's own walls
  • Vintage trade-sign typography
  • Call and book paths built for a phone in a pocket

Demo build · Market kitchen · Alberta · No website before

A poster you can order from.

A family phở stall in a farmers' market, invisible online. The demo prints its story like a Saigon poster: red-ink duotones on aged paper, postal stamps for frames. The family's real bowls arrive in full colour, right before the phone number.

  • Red-ink duotone imagery on an aged-paper ground
  • The family's real cooking as the only full-colour payoff
  • Call-to-order path built for a market counter
(03) Straight answers

Asked, answered.

Can my business get a build like these?

That's the entire offer: a custom site at this standard from $499, live in 48 hours once we have your content and payment. The price is published, and what moves it is scope: page count, whether we write the copy, extras like booking or e-commerce; the services page lays it out plainly. Every build is designed from scratch for the business, fast on a phone, and yours to keep outright at handover. The process page walks through exactly how the two days run.

How long would a build like this take?

48 hours from the moment your content and payment are both in. Before that, there's one short fit call where we agree on the scope and put the price in writing. If you need a week to gather your photos and text, the clock simply waits.

Can you rebuild a website I already have?

Yes, and the before-and-after sliders above show exactly what that looks like. A rebuild is often the fastest project there is, because the words and photos already exist. Send us the link and we'll tell you plainly what we'd keep and what we'd change.
